# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The student presenting this B.Tech major project to examiners. They stand at a
screen and must make a live system legible to a skeptical audience in under
30 seconds. A secondary audience is the teammate operating the demo.

## Product Purpose

Show that a real AI loop is running end to end: a moisture probe sends live
readings over WiFi, a Random Forest predicts soil moisture 15 minutes ahead, a
decision is produced, and a pump responds within hard safety limits. Success
is an examiner who can see fresh data, the current AI state and the prediction
change without being told they are real.

## Positioning

Every step of the inference is visible on one screen — state chain, raw input,
forecast, decision explanation, safety status and device-reported pump state —
rather than a black-box prediction.

## Operating Context

College lab demo on a laptop and ESP32 on the same WiFi. One capacitive soil
moisture probe and one 5 V submersible pump are the only hardware. Temperature
and humidity are fetched from Open-Meteo by the server. Readings are logged
every 60 s to SQLite. Pump safety (60 s burst, 45 min gap, sanity cutoff)
lives in firmware.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live telemetry, 15-minute regression forecast, AUTO/MANUAL pump control.
- Dashboard must update without a page reload.
- No extra sensors, no cloud account, no new frontend dependencies or CDN.
- Demo must work from simulated history that ends at the current minute until
  real sensor logging has run long enough.
- No fabricated confidence: show forest spread plus held-out MAE/R².

## Evidence on Hand

`reports/`, `src/model.py`, `src/server.py`, and the design/plan docs under
`docs/superpowers/`. No real field dataset exists yet; the initial history is
generated by `src/simulator.py` and is labelled as such in the UI.

## Product Principles

1. Freshness is the proof: always show the timestamp and age of the last reading.
2. Show the chain, not just the answer: state → input → forecast → decision → pump.
3. Never imply safety came from software; the device enforces it.
4. Say when data is simulated or stale instead of hiding it.
5. One screen, one glance, no navigation during a demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, visible focus states, and no meaning carried by colour alone
(state names are written out).

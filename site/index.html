One decision, four steps

The server reads the probe every three seconds, adds the weather from Open-Meteo, then predicts the next fifteen minutes. It sends at most one timed command.

  1. 01 — Read

    Moisture from the capacitive probe, temperature and humidity from Open-Meteo.

  2. 02 — Forecast

    A Random Forest estimates the change in moisture over the next fifteen minutes.

  3. 03 — Decide

    AUTO queues one timed burst below 30 percent. MANUAL waits for a click.

  4. 04 — Guard

    The ESP32 checks every command against local limits before it powers the pump.

Four limits the ESP32 keeps

Every command carries a duration, and the firmware can refuse it. The same limits apply in AUTO and MANUAL.

  • 60 slongest single burst, then the pump stops
  • 45 minshortest gap between two bursts
  • 5–95%plausible range for a probe reading
  • 30 soffline fallback after three failed contacts
